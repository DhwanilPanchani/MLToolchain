Key takeaways:
  • QNN FP32 on Hexagon NPU is 10× faster than ONNX FP32 on Apple M-series CPU.
  • QNN INT8 is 150× faster than ONNX INT8 on Mac (37,600 µs → 251 µs).
  • INT8 quantization delivers a 1.42× speedup on Hexagon vs FP32 on Hexagon.
  • 100% of compute ops dispatched to NPU — zero CPU fallback ops.
  • INT8 adds 4 extra quantize/dequantize ops in the graph (101 vs 97).

Notes:
  - Mac latency: ONNX Runtime CPUExecutionProvider, 100 runs, p50.
  - Hexagon latency: QNN runtime, 100 runs, warm p50 (first cold run excluded).
  - Peak memory is higher on Hexagon because activations and weights are pinned
    in VTCM (Vector TCM) for zero-copy NPU access.
